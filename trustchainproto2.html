A case-first workflow

Start with one item, then add related evidence to the same case. TrustChain keeps a timeline, lets you record your own context, and creates a redacted brief you can review before sharing. Pattern matches are clues to investigate, not proof.

How TrustChain works

1
Identify

Detects whether the evidence is a message, link, screenshot, QR code, document, number or ID.

2
Extract

Reads available image text, QR payloads and selectable PDF text; extraction can be incomplete.

3
Analyze & score

Rule-based checks look for impersonation, urgency, credential requests and suspicious domains.

4
Explain

Each displayed heuristic point comes from a fixed-weight indicator; the total is not a probability.

Analysis runs in your browser. The app does not directly send submissions to its own server; external scripts are loaded from CDNs and are trusted dependencies. The 0-100 values are heuristic points, not probabilities, and are not checked against a live threat-intelligence database, always use your own judgment alongside this tool.
